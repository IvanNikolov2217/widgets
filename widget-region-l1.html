L1 constraint region — the diamond
allowed region (|w₁| + |w₂| ≤ c) corners on the axes

The L1 region is a diamond: every weight pair inside satisfies |w₁| + |w₂| ≤ c. Its four sharp corners lie exactly on the axes — at those points one weight is exactly zero. Because the corners stick out furthest along the axes, the solution often lands on one, eliminating a weight entirely.
